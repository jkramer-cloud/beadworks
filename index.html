How to use a soroban

Each rod is a place value. Slide beads toward the middle beam by clicking or tapping them. The top bead is worth five; each bottom bead is worth one. Beads away from the beam do not count. Click a lower bead to move it and all beads between it and the beam. Click an active bead to move it and the beads farther from the beam away.

Keyboard: Tab to a bead, then press Enter or Space. On a focused column, use Up and Down to change its digit.
